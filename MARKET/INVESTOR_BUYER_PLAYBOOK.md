# FactLedger — Investor and Strategic Buyer Playbook

This playbook defines how to handle financing and acquisition interest without giving away leverage or making unsupported claims.

## Investor intake

For every inbound investor, record firm/person, stage, check size, thesis, prior Solana/crypto investments, decision process, expected timeline, and whether they lead or follow.

Before sharing sensitive information, send the public one-pager and demo. Move to diligence only after confirming fit.

## Financing offer comparison

Compare offers on economic and control terms, not headline amount alone:

- cash invested;
- pre-money/post-money valuation or SAFE cap/discount;
- dilution on an as-converted basis;
- pro-rata rights;
- board/information rights;
- liquidation preference;
- token warrant or token rights;
- founder vesting/re-vesting;
- exclusivity/no-shop;
- closing conditions;
- legal fees;
- strategic value and follow-on capacity.

No financing document should be signed without owner approval and qualified legal review.

## Buyer intake

For strategic acquisition interest, determine first whether the buyer wants technology, IP, data/intelligence assets, customers/distribution, team, or the entire company. Ask for a written indication of interest before granting deep diligence access.

## Acquisition offer comparison

Evaluate:

- cash at close;
- stock/rollover equity;
- earn-out amount, measurement and duration;
- escrow/holdback;
- treatment of grants and outstanding financing;
- IP and data transfer scope;
- customer-contract assignment;
- employment/consulting obligations;
- non-compete/non-solicit restrictions where lawful;
- representations, warranties and indemnity caps;
- tax consequences reviewed by advisers;
- certainty and timing of close.

## Negotiation posture

Do not broadly market FactLedger as distressed or 'for sale'. Build competitive tension where possible. The strongest negotiating leverage is reproducible production proof, proprietary intelligence history, active integrations, recurring revenue, and multiple credible financing/buyer alternatives.

## Evidence discipline

Never inflate users, revenue, dataset size, uptime, grant status, partnerships, or live integrations. Share sourced metrics with date ranges and definitions. Forecasts and pipeline must be labeled clearly.

## Decision threshold

A strategic sale should be compared against the expected value of continuing independently with available grants/investment. A higher headline price is not automatically better if most consideration is uncertain earn-out, restrictive employment, or weak closing certainty.
