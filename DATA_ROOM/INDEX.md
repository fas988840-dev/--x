# FactLedger — Data Room Index

The data room should make FactLedger financeable and acquisition-ready without exposing secrets or unsupported claims. Populate each section with source documents as they become real.

## 01 Corporate and ownership

- legal entity formation documents, if any;
- cap table and founder ownership;
- board/shareholder approvals where applicable;
- tax registrations and banking documents shared only through controlled diligence channels.

## 02 Intellectual property

- repository ownership and contribution history;
- employee/contractor invention-assignment agreements, if applicable;
- dependency and open-source license inventory;
- trademark/domain assets;
- list of proprietary modules and data rights.

## 03 Product and technology

- architecture diagram;
- API documentation and endpoint inventory;
- Evidence/Risk Graph documentation;
- MCP/agent documentation;
- deployment architecture;
- production/demo URLs once verified;
- roadmap and technical debt register.

## 04 Security and reliability

- CI/CodeQL evidence;
- test counts and build evidence from current runs;
- dependency-audit reports and remediation decisions;
- authentication, rate-limit, CORS, logging, and secret-management controls;
- incident and vulnerability history when applicable;
- uptime/SLA data only after measured in production.

## 05 Data and intelligence assets

- Evidence/Risk Graph schema/versioning;
- provenance and confidence rules;
- retention and deletion policy;
- data-source licenses/terms;
- measured dataset size and coverage only when durable storage exists;
- privacy/customer-data handling policy.

## 06 Customers and market

- design-partner pipeline;
- pilot agreements and customer contracts;
- customer references where authorized;
- product usage metrics;
- pricing history and sales funnel;
- churn/retention data when real.

## 07 Finance

- monthly P&L/cash movement once the project has activity;
- infrastructure and vendor costs;
- MRR/ARR and invoice/payment records;
- grant receipts and milestone evidence;
- forecast assumptions separated from actuals.

## 08 Funding

- grant applications and decisions;
- SAFE/equity/token documents;
- investor updates;
- use-of-funds plans;
- financing offers and term-sheet comparison notes.

## 09 Acquisition readiness

- strategic buyer thesis;
- acquisition teaser;
- buyer/contact pipeline;
- normalized operating metrics;
- IP/data transfer constraints;
- change-of-control clauses in customer/vendor agreements;
- management transition and retention considerations.

## Diligence rules

No private key, seed phrase, API token, password, customer secret, or unrestricted identity document belongs in the public repository. Sensitive diligence materials must be shared through access-controlled channels. Every metric should have a source and date; projections must be labeled as projections.
